Rules:

1. FESTAFORA 2026 only.
2. No RISE/LUMINUX naming in the app.
3. Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS only.
4. No React/Vue/Angular/Next.
5. No backend required.
6. Use localStorage for demo persistence.
7. Use Chart.js for charts.
8. Simulation must feel physically plausible, not random noise.
9. AI recommendation must be actionable and beginner-friendly.
10. AI demo must be honestly labeled as demo/rule engine.
11. Marketplace price must use actual benchmark sources when available.
12. 30% means gross margin, calculated as cost/0.70.
13. Do not fabricate marketplace sources.
14. Education is a first-class product feature.
15. Focus UX on “what is happening?” and “what should I do?”
16. Keep sustainability as a meaningful but secondary purchase driver, consistent with the consumer research.
17. Do not claim that bagasse media is already scientifically validated for hydroponic water unless actual evidence is supplied.
18. Do not claim production AI prediction model exists unless actual model evidence is supplied.
19. Finish with QA and a working app, not scaffolding only.
