# SOURCE NOTES

## Primary source

The attached README is the source for social listening insights.

Important source facts:
- research period 7-8 October 2026
- data frozen 7 October 2026 21:32 UTC
- 136,019 unique public records
- 93,125 gold records
- 91,068 customer voice records
- 8 platforms
- 677 academic publications collected
- 7 expert/institution sources

## Core consumer insights

1. Biggest issue: plants becoming unhealthy or dying.
2. Tutorials and community are the strongest explicit demand.
3. pH and TDS/EC monitoring are strongly requested.
4. Diagnosis and dashboard are useful.
5. Bagasse is rarely mentioned by customers.
6. Sustainability is less prominent than price/cost in purchase-related discussion.
7. DIY is a serious competitor.
8. Passive systems are a real alternative.
9. Subscription/app dependency can create friction.
10. Age/income/location assumptions cannot be inferred reliably from public conversation.

## Important methodological limitations

- not a population survey
- YouTube dominates Indonesian records
- Reddit came through a public archive
- marketplace reviews were not accessible in the research corpus
- intent classifier performance is moderate
- personas are conversation segments, not actual people
- percentages should be used carefully

## Historical-context warning

Some lines in the source README mention RISE and other older competition contexts. Those are historical context only. For the HYCANE app in this package, use FESTAFORA 2026 as the competition context and do not import the old competition names or themes.
