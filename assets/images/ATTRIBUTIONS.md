## Google Stitch design assets

`assets/images/stitch/*.jpg` (47 images + `s01-login-bg.jpg`) were downloaded from the image URLs embedded in the project's Google Stitch export (`stitch_hycane_smart_hydroponic_ecosystem/*/code.html`) so the demo works offline. They are design-tool generated visuals supplied with the HYCANE design, used here as product/crop illustrations for the demo. File names map to the Stitch screen number (e.g. `s08-15.jpg` = screen 08 Marketplace).

## Official HYCANE logo

`assets/images/brand/` holds the official logo supplied by the team (`Minimal Green Leaf HYCANE Logo.png`, kept as `hycane-logo-original.png`) and variants generated from it without redrawing: trimmed full logo (`hycane-logo.png`), white version for dark backgrounds (`hycane-logo-white.png`), leaf mark only (`hycane-mark.png`, `hycane-mark-white.png`) and favicon (`favicon-64.png`).

## Other assets

Products without a Stitch photo use original inline SVG illustrations generated by `js/art.js` (no external source).

Icons are inline SVG drawn in the style of Lucide (ISC license) in `js/icons.js` (inline instead of the Stitch Material Symbols webfont so icons work offline).

Chart.js 4.4.1 (MIT) is vendored at `assets/vendor/chart.umd.min.js`.
